TechNova Corp is an artificial intelligence company founded by Elena Rostova in 2021. The company is headquartered in Austin, Texas. 

In 2023, TechNova Corp released its flagship model called Nebula-7. Nebula-7 was trained using cloud infrastructure provided by SkyNet Systems. 

Marcus Vance joined TechNova Corp as Chief Technology Officer in 2024. Before joining TechNova, Marcus Vance worked at DataCore Labs as a Principal Researcher. DataCore Labs is a direct competitor to TechNova Corp and is based in Seattle.
